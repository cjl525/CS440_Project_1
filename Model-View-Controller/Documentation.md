### Model View Controller Architecture

---

## Model

### Data and Logic  
**Database:** PostgreSQL  

The model handles all SQL queries directed by the controller.

---

## Controller

### Requests and Responses  
**Server:** Express.js  

The controller is where the bulk of the logic exists, and where routes are stored.

---

## View

### The traditional "front end" of the application.  
It is what displays the content that the controller creates.
